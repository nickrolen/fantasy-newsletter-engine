### **9. Around the NBA**

**Around the NBA**

- **Doncic Drops 41 in Heartbreaking Loss at Phoenix**

Luka Doncic torched the short-handed Suns for 41 points on 12-of-21 shooting — including 6-of-11 from deep — to go with eight rebounds and eight assists on Feb 26, but a Royce O'Neale three-pointer with 0.9 seconds left sank the Lakers 113-110. It was the kind of performance that would have been the story of the night in any other context, but in fantasy terms, it was the game that clinched Nick's sixth title. Doncic's 71.0 FP in this outing was the third-best single-game performance of the week, and his 236.40 FP across 4 games earned him his third POTW award this season. Since returning from a four-game absence due to a left hamstring strain, Doncic has averaged 30.4 points, 9.0 assists, and 6.8 rebounds per game — the kind of run that makes you forget he missed time at all.

- **Sengun Posts Triple-Double in Rockets' Rout of Kings**

Alperen Sengun stuffed the stat sheet against Sacramento on Feb 25, posting 26 points, 13 rebounds, and 11 assists in Houston's 128-97 blowout victory — his third triple-double of the season. The performance generated 72.65 FP, the single highest total of any player this week and the headline entry on Garrett's stat sheet. Reed Sheppard started in place of Amen Thompson, who sat out with a quad issue, and Kevin Durant added 21 points. For Garrett, Sengun's elite two-way production has been one of the week's biggest differentiators — his 161.70 FP over 4 games anchored a center rotation that outscored Hayden's by 289 FP.

- **Cunningham and Duren Power Pistons Past OKC in Conference Leaders Clash**

Cunningham's 62.3 FP outing was the best single performance of his week, and the two-way showcase earned praise from analysts who noted he became just the fourth player in history to record at least 29 points, five rebounds, 13 assists, three steals, and three blocks in one game. For Benton, Cunningham's 195.90 FP across 4 games anchored the effort that came within 15.05 points of delaying Nick's title clinch. On the other side of that matchup, Duren's 181.05 FP over 4 games on Nick's roster was a quiet but critical contribution — his rebounding and interior scoring gave Luka my Balls the center production edge that ultimately made the difference.

- **Giannis Nearing Return After Five-Week Absence**

Giannis Antetokounmpo is expected to return Monday night against the Boston Celtics after missing the last five-plus weeks with a right calf strain, per Shams Charania of ESPN. The two-time MVP last played on Jan 23 in a loss to the Denver Nuggets, and his absence has loomed large over Garrett's roster all season. Antetokounmpo has logged just 50.8% of available games with a 42.21 FPPG average — production that's been sorely missed. Garrett's 24.9% season injury burden owes a significant debt to the Giannis situation, and his return could be the spark that extends a 3-game win streak. Milwaukee teammates Ryan Rollins (Nick) and Kevin Porter Jr. (Hayden) have both thrived in expanded roles during the absence.

- **Booker Trending Toward Early March Return from Hip Strain**

Devin Booker has been out for four games with a hip strain suffered in the Suns' loss to San Antonio on Feb 19, but reports indicate he is progressing well and could return for either the Sacramento game on Tuesday or Chicago on Thursday, according to John Gambadoro of Arizona Sports. The timing matters for Hayden, who has navigated a league-high 26.5% season injury burden and enters Week 19 at just 74.0% health — the lowest in the league. Booker's 33.43 FPPG on the season understates his impact when healthy, and getting him back alongside Pascal Siakam, who is also expected to return from a wrist injury, would give Hayden his healthiest lineup in weeks. Whether it's enough to avoid a 15th loss against a -1365 moneyline versus Nick is another story entirely.
