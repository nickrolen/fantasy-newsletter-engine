**Luka my Balls 1910.20 def. Saboner 1778.90**

*Saboner (9-11): 46 games, 38.67 fppg (G: 37.80 | F: 41.20 | C: 38.40)*
*Season series: Nick 6, Garrett 1 | All-time: Nick leads 46-20*

Last week's Betting Lines billed this as a pick'em — Nick a -105 moneyline favorite with just a 9.5-point spread — and for most of the week, it lived up to the billing. Garrett actually posted the higher team FPPG (38.67 to 38.20), squeezing more per-game production out of his roster than the six-time champion. The result snapped Garrett's 4-game win streak, the longest of his season and a run that had included wins over the top two teams in consecutive weeks. Nick now owns 46 of the 66 all-time meetings between these two — a staggering 70% win rate that remains the league's most lopsided rivalry. With both teams trending hot entering the week (Nick 9% above his season average, Garrett 11% above his), the matchup delivered on its billing as a high-scoring affair — 3689.10 combined points — even if the final margin wasn't as razor-thin as the oddsmakers expected.
